# Northstar Community Lab

Northstar Community Lab is a volunteer makerspace in Cedar Point. Maya Chen maintains its public tool catalogue. Owen Patel maintains the laser cutter and checks the ventilation filters.

A heavy rainstorm damaged the roof above the fabrication room. Maya closed that room and moved the repair workshop to the ground-floor classroom. Owen and building manager Lena Ortiz found a blocked drain above the east wall. The electronics bench and lending desk stayed open in the west wing. No tools were damaged; the laser cutter remained offline until the roof repair and ventilation check were complete.

Members can borrow portable soldering kits for seven days. They reserve kits through the catalogue, collect them at the lending desk, and return them there. Maya updates the catalogue; Owen does not manage loans. The repair workshop teaches plug replacement, fuse testing, and damaged-cable identification, but does not certify mains-wiring repairs.

After the roof work, Lena recorded the repair date. Owen checked the filters and tested the laser cutter before Maya reopened the fabrication room. The workshop was rescheduled for Saturday.

The lab uses a shared maintenance log for room closures, equipment checks, and repair dates. Lena records building work in the log, while Owen records machine inspections. Maya updates the public catalogue after equipment is cleared for use, so members can see which tools are available before reserving them.

The lending desk coordinates portable-kit returns with the electronics bench. Staff inspect returned soldering kits for missing leads and damaged tips, then Maya updates the catalogue if a kit needs repair. These kit checks do not authorize reopening the fabrication room; the roof repair and Owen's ventilation check are separate conditions for bringing the laser cutter back online.
