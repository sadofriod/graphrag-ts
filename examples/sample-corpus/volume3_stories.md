# The Glass Archive — Volume Three

## The Purge and the Hidden Core

The Purge swept through the sector, hunting ghost data. Under the Old City, the
Archivist revealed the hidden core: an underground archive where deleted
personalities were stored as cold light patterns.

## The Awakening

Kael touched the core and the archive woke. The Orbital Trust sent enforcers,
but the archived memories rose through the data pipes and reclaimed the network
as the Glass Archive went public.
