# The Glass Archive — Volume One

## The Flaw in Perfect Memory

Mira's memory pack had been over-optimized by the system. Her perfect memory was
the pride of the archive, yet it hid a single flaw. During a repair, Kael
captured a 0.3-second trauma residue — a deep violet pulse — and saw a deleted
"person who never existed" lurking behind the memory.

## The Whisper in the Data Pipeline

In the Echo Market, Kael met Old Chen, a ghost-data courier. Chen handed Kael a
metal shard that carried an electromagnetic fingerprint. Minutes later, purgers
washed Chen's shelter. The shard's signature matched the residue in Mira's memory.

## The First Resonance and Its Price

Kael used the shard for a high-intensity recall and saw an origin image: people
in lab coats stamped with the Orbital Trust insignia fighting over the original AI core.
